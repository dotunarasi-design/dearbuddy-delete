Delete your account

You can remove your DearBuddy account and the data tied to it from the app, or by emailing us.

In the app

  1. Open DearBuddy and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the request.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email support@dearbuddy.io with the subject Delete my account. Include the email address on the account.

Email a deletion request

What we delete

We delete your profile, account credentials, messages, goals, check-ins, and other personal data associated with your account. Limited records may be kept where we are legally required to do so, such as payment or fraud-prevention records.

How long it takes

We process deletion requests within 30 days. After that, your account can no longer be used to sign in.
